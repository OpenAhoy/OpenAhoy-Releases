# Model settings brand assets

`minimax-color-black.png` is the unmodified horizontal BrandColor_Black.png from the [MiniMax official logo asset pack](https://file.cdn.minimax.io/public/brand-vi/20260914/MiniMax_Logo_Asset.zip). See the [MiniMax brand guidelines and usage terms](https://www.minimax.io/brand-vi#usage-terms). The mark identifies the MiniMax H3 credential provider.

The provider logos on the model overview identify the service configured by each card.

- `ark.svg` is the icon referenced by the [Volcengine Ark console](https://console.volcengine.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/overview).
- `minimax-icon.png` is from the [MiniMax site icon](https://www.minimax.io/favicon.ico), shared by the mainland China and international cards.
- `autodl.png` comes from the [AutoDL site icon](https://www.autodl.com/favicon.png).
- `tokenhex.jpg` is the logo declared by [Token Hex's public site metadata](https://api.tokenhex.com/api/status).
- `openai-black.svg` and `openai-white.svg` are the Blossom artwork from the [OpenAI logo pack](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip).
- Suno reuses the existing `assets/management/suno-favicon.ico`.
- `ace-music.png` is based on the [ACE Music site icon](https://acemusic.ai/public/assets/favicon-192.png).
